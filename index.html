Professional Summary

Mechanical design and quality engineer who connects what is drawn to what is actually built, inspected and delivered.

I have four years of hands-on CAD/CAE experience in manufacturing, rail and architectural product environments. My design work covers SolidWorks, Creo, AutoCAD, ANSYS and Mastercam, backed by stress and tolerance analysis, GD&T, DFMA and FMEA.

On the quality side, I have reviewed engineering calculations, built and monitored test set-ups, validated plating and tooling systems, and supported audit-ready documentation under ISO 9001, AS9100 and Nadcap. I hold Canadian credentials and am seeking a mechanical design, quality or process engineering role in the Greater Toronto Area.
